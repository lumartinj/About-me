Phase 01 · Concept & Architecture

Next-Generation Aircraft Program

Architectural definition and MBSE strategy for a future single-aisle aircraft programme.

Specific technical tasks

  • Took ownership of the Product Breakdown Structure (PBS) for a future commercial aircraft programme, defining and organising architectural layers within an Agile framework.
  • Established Cabin Installation Design Principles for Potable Water, Waste System, Cargo Loading System and Seat-to-Seat Power.
  • Conducted functional allocation trades for a Cabin & Cargo architecture that enables deep customisation while limiting industrial impact, reducing lead times and accommodating late changes.
  • Aligned system architects, industrial teams and the commercial offer team, keeping the architecture scalable for future cross-programme wide bodies.
  • Decomposed all Cabin & Cargo functions down to Level 3, with clear interfaces to the respective design offices.
  • Evaluated top-level aircraft functions, proposed updates for Cabin & Cargo needs and drove alignment between domain architects and aircraft-level stakeholders.
  • Generated SRDs automatically from the functional requirements in the Cameo model.

Key achievements

  • From support to ownership: took over the PBS and architectural alignment, driving foundational decisions in the early concept phase.
  • Unified the MBSE deliverable pipeline: turned a complex tool and stakeholder landscape into a clear map from system models to required deliverables, addressing community upskilling needs.
